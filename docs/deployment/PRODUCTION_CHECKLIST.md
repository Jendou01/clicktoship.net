No production release or business activation is authorized by Phase17. Checkboxes require named approval and attached evidence, not template existence.

- [ ] Offsite authenticated encrypted DB/object backups, escrow, retention/deletion/PITR, approved RPO/RTO, representative restore and cross-version image rollback.
- [ ] Private scanning/quarantine/download encryption/access/residency/retention and cleanup validated; keep document/export operations closed until complete.
- [ ] Aramex official current contracts/account/service permissions, authorized sandbox, create/reference/unknown/label/pickup/tracking/webhook evidence, duplicate/weight discrepancy/reconciliation and independent live activation signoff. **No external call made.**

- [ ] Shopyzee actual deployed Nazmart source/version, plugin lifecycle/model/auth/tenant contracts, independently reviewed per-store consent/rotation/replay connector and isolated sync evidence. **No live installation/global store key.**

- [ ] Capacity/soak/fairness and critical worker reservation measured on host; monitoring, backup/TLS/disk/heartbeat, incident/on-call, privacy and formal accessibility/user acceptance approved.

No flags or administrative role can waive evidence gates. Keep unknown shipping holds until verified independent reconciliation; no automatic finance/provider replay, no fabricated revenue/carrier/FX evidence, no standing cross-tenant privilege. Customer notifications require separate approval.
